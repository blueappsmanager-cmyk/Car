Executive Summary: Car Clash 3D does not collect, track, harvest, or sell any personal information. Third-party advertising partners may process standard non-identifying technical identifiers strictly to serve rewarded in-game ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile game application Car Clash 3D (Package Name: com.blueapps.carclash), developed and published by BlueAppBox ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent, privacy-first environment. This policy complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Car Clash 3D does NOT collect, harvest, store, share, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). When you download, install, and play this game:

  • We do not require user accounts, email sign-ups, or social logins.
  • We do not collect your real name, physical address, email address, or telephone number.
  • We do not access your device contacts, address book, photo gallery, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not track or access your precise GPS location or real-time physical coordinates.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers, banking credentials, or biometric identifiers.

3. Local Device Storage (Offline Gameplay Data)

All gameplay progress and game settings are stored exclusively on your local device (using standard browser HTML5 localStorage / private application sandbox storage):

  • Campaign Progression: Completed stage index (Levels 1 to 300), highest unlocked stage, and star rating records (1–3 stars per level).
  • Virtual Game Balances: In-game earned stars and remaining hint tokens.
  • Player Preferences: Sound effects on/off toggle, active visual environment theme (Sunny Terminal, Midnight City, Wood Depot), and unlocked vehicle liveries in the Car Fleet Garage.

Notice: This data never leaves your client device and is never uploaded to any external server or cloud database under our control.

4. Hardware APIs & Device Permissions

Car Clash 3D uses the following non-sensitive APIs strictly for core in-game functionality:

  • Web Audio API: Generates procedural sound effects (engine accelerations, vehicle bumper recoil bumps, victory fanfare) mathematically in real-time. No microphone input is ever monitored or recorded.
  • Vibration API (Haptics): Delivers subtle tactile feedback during bumper collisions and level completions. This can be muted at any time through your device system vibration settings.
  • Android System Navigation Bridge: Coordinates standard Android hardware back-button navigation and rewarded ad display callbacks without harvesting hardware serial numbers, IMEI, or MAC addresses.

5. Third-Party Services & Rewarded Advertisements

Car Clash 3D is free to play and supported by optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to perform an untangle shuffle when traffic is locked or to earn a free hint). These ads are delivered by certified third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob or certified ad partners).

These advertising networks may process pseudonymous, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry strictly for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance metrics:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system build
  • General coarse geographic region (country or city level derived from IP)
  • Ad view, completion, and fraud prevention signals
How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:
You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Car Clash 3D is a family-friendly casual car jam puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We strictly adhere to COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • When child-directed settings or Families Policy rules apply, only contextual, non-personalized, family-safe advertisements certified under Google Play guidelines are served.
  • Parents or guardians with any questions or concerns may contact us at blueappsmanager@gmail.com for immediate assistance.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Because all gameplay data is stored solely on your device, you have complete control over its retention:

  1. In-App Reset: Open Settings inside Car Clash 3D and tap "Reset All Progress to Level 1" to instantly purge all records, stars, and customizations.
  2. Device Clear Data: In Android, navigate to: Settings → Apps → Car Clash 3D → Storage → Clear Data & Cache.
  3. App Uninstall: Removing the app from your device automatically and permanently erases all associated local files.
